About

I’m a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Otago, New Zealand. I work on generative recommendation, with a focus on semantic IDs and large language models.

News

  • Aug 2026Our paper on faithful evaluation of semantic-ID tokenizers was accepted to CIKM 2026.
  • Apr 2026Our preprint on complementary-aware sequential recommendation (CAST) was posted to arXiv.
  • Jul 2025Our paper on semantic-relation-guided dual-view contrastive learning for session-based recommendation was accepted to ACM TOIS.
  • Jun 2025I presented our work on LLM-inferred item relationships for sequential recommendation at the AIRA 2025 Mid-Year Workshop.
